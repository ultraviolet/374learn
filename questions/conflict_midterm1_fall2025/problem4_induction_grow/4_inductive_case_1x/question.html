Consider the following recursively defined function: $$\mathsf{grow}(w) = \begin{cases} \varepsilon & \text{if } w = \varepsilon \\ \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{0}x \\ \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{1}x \end{cases}$$ We want to prove: $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))| = 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w)$ for all $w \in \{0,1\}^*$. **Induction hypothesis:** For all strings $x$ with $|x| < |w|$, $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))| = 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, x) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, x)$. Now consider the **inductive case**: $w = \mathbf{1}x$ for some string $x$. *(Note: Since $|x| < |w|$, we may apply the induction hypothesis to $x$.)* Put the following steps in the correct order to prove this case. $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))| = |\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}x))| \quad$ (because $w = \mathbf{1}x$) $= |\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x))| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}x) = \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x)$) $= |\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{0}y) = \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(y)$ and $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}y) = \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(y)$, with $y = \mathsf{grow}(x)$) $= 3 + |\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))| \quad$ (by definition of $|\cdot|$: $|\mathbf{101} \cdot z| = 3 + |z|$) $= 3 + 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, x) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, x) \quad$ (by induction hypothesis applied to $x$) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, x) + 3 \cdot (1 + \#(\mathbf{1}, x)) \quad$ (arithmetic) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{1}x) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{1}x) \quad$ (by definition of $\#$: $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{1}x) = \#(\mathbf{0}, x)$, $\#(\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{1}x) = 1 + \#(\mathbf{1}, x)$) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w) \quad$ (because $w = \mathbf{1}x$) $= |\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}x))| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}x) = \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}x)$) $= 2 + |\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))| \quad$ (by definition of $|\cdot|$: $|\mathbf{01} \cdot z| = 2 + |z|$) $= 3 + 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w) \quad$ (by induction hypothesis applied to $w$)
