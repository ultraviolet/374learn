Consider the following recursively defined function: $$\mathsf{grow}(w) = \begin{cases} \varepsilon & \text{if } w = \varepsilon \\ \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{0}x \\ \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{1}x \end{cases}$$ We want to prove: $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))| = 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w)$ for all $w \in \{0,1\}^*$. **Induction hypothesis:** For all strings $x$ with $|x| < |w|$, $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))| = 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, x) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, x)$. Now consider the **base case**: $w = \varepsilon$. Put the following steps in the correct order to prove the base case. $|\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))| = |\mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(\varepsilon))| \quad$ (because $w = \varepsilon$) $= |\mathsf{grow}(\varepsilon)| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\varepsilon) = \varepsilon$) $= |\varepsilon| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\varepsilon) = \varepsilon$) $= 0 \quad$ (by definition of $|\cdot|$: $|\varepsilon| = 0$) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, \varepsilon) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, \varepsilon) \quad$ (since $\#(\mathbf{0}, \varepsilon) = \#(\mathbf{1}, \varepsilon) = 0$, so $2 \cdot 0 + 3 \cdot 0 = 0$) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w) \quad$ (because $w = \varepsilon$) $= |\mathsf{grow}(w)| \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$) $= 2 \cdot \#(\mathbf{0}, w) + 3 \cdot \#(\mathbf{1}, w) \quad$ (by induction hypothesis) $= |\varepsilon| + |\varepsilon| \quad$ (by applying $\mathsf{grow}$ twice to $\varepsilon$)
