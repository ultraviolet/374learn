Provide a **regular expression** for the following language over the alphabet $\{A, B, N\}$: ***All strings in $\{A, B, N\}^*$ where every $N$ is directly between two $A$s.*** Formally, for every occurrence of $N$ at position $i$ in the string, position $i-1$ must be $A$ and position $i+1$ must be $A$. This language contains $\varepsilon$, $ANA$, $BANANA$, and $ABAAANANABBB$, but does **not** contain $ANBAN$ (the first $N$ is followed by $B$, not $A$) or $BAANNANA$ (the first $N$ is followed by another $N$, not $A$).
**Regex format:** Use `+` for union, `*` for Kleene star, `e` for $\varepsilon$, and parentheses for grouping. Concatenation is implicit. The alphabet is $\{A, B, N\}$. (B*(A(NA)*)*)*
