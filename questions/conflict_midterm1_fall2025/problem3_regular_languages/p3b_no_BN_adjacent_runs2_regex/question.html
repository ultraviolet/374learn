Provide a **regular expression** for the following language over the alphabet $\{A, B, N\}$: ***All strings in $\{A, B, N\}^*$ in which $B$ and $N$ are never adjacent, and every maximal run of identical symbols has length at most $2$.*** This language contains $\varepsilon$, $A$, $BB$, $NN$, $ANA$, $BAN$, and $BAANNANA$, but does **not** contain $ANBAN$ ($B$ and $N$ are adjacent) or $ABAAANANABBB$ (has a run of $3$ consecutive $A$s). *(Note: a "run" of symbol $X$ is a maximal consecutive sequence of $X$s.)*
**Regex format:** Use `+` for union, `*` for Kleene star, `e` for $\varepsilon$, and parentheses for grouping. Concatenation is implicit. The alphabet is $\{A, B, N\}$. (e+A+AA)((B+BB+N+NN)(A+AA))*(e+B+BB+N+NN)
