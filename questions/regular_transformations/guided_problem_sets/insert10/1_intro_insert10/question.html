For regular language transformation problems, it's important that you fully understand the transformation in question. A good first step is to think about a very simple language and consider what strings are accepted by the transformed language. Consider the following language transformation: $$\mathsf{INSERT10}(L) := \{ x10y \mid xy \in L \}$$
Suppose that $L = \{ 01 \}$. What strings are contained in $\mathsf{INSERT10}(L)$? 1001 0101 0110 1010 0011 1100 **Explanation:** We need strings that can be formed by inserting $10$ into the string $01$. - $1001$ ✓ (insert $10$ before $01$: $\varepsilon \cdot 10 \cdot 01$) - $0101$ ✓ (insert $10$ between the $0$ and $1$: $0 \cdot 10 \cdot 1$) - $0110$ ✓ (insert $10$ after $01$: $01 \cdot 10 \cdot \varepsilon$) - $1010$ ✗ (cannot insert $10$ into $01$ and get $1010$) - $0011$ ✗ (cannot insert $10$ into $01$ and get $0011$) - $1100$ ✗ (cannot insert $10$ into $01$ and get $1100$)
