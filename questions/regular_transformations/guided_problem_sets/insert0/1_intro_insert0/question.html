For regular language transformation problems, it's important that you fully understand the transformation in question. A good first step is to think about a very simple language and consider what strings are accepted by the transformed language. Consider the following language transformation: $$\mathsf{INSERT0}(L) := \{ x0y \mid xy \in L \}$$
Suppose that $L = \{ 11 \}$. What strings are contained in $\mathsf{INSERT0}(L)$? 011 101 110 111 001 010 **Explanation:** We need strings that can be formed by inserting a single $0$ into the string $11$. - $011$ ✓ (insert $0$ before $11$: $\varepsilon \cdot 0 \cdot 11$) - $101$ ✓ (insert $0$ between the two $1$s: $1 \cdot 0 \cdot 1$) - $110$ ✓ (insert $0$ after $11$: $11 \cdot 0 \cdot \varepsilon$) - $111$ ✗ (cannot insert $0$ into $11$ and get $111$) - $001$ ✗ (would require inserting $00$, not a single $0$) - $010$ ✗ (cannot insert a single $0$ into $11$ and get $010$)
