Provide a **regular expression** for the following language over the alphabet $\{I, L, N\}$: ***All strings in $\{I, L, N\}^*$ where every run of $L$s has length exactly $2$.*** This language contains $\varepsilon$, $ILLINI$, $ILLILLI$, and $INILLILLININI$, but does **not** contain $ILINNI$ (has a run of length 1) or $NILLLLL$ (has a run of length 5). *(Note: a "run" of $L$s is a maximal consecutive sequence of $L$s.)*
**Regex format:** Use `+` for union, `*` for Kleene star, `e` for $\varepsilon$, and parentheses for grouping. Concatenation is implicit. The alphabet is $\{I, L, N\}$. (I+N)*(LL(I+N)(I+N)*)*(LL+e)
