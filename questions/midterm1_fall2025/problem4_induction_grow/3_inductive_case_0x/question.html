Consider the following recursively defined function: $$\mathsf{grow}(w) = \begin{cases} \varepsilon & \text{if } w = \varepsilon \\ \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{0}x \\ \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x) & \text{if } w = \mathbf{1}x \end{cases}$$ We want to prove: $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))) \geq \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(w))$ for all $w$. **Induction hypothesis:** For all strings $x$ with $|x| < |w|$, $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))) \geq \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(x))$. Now consider the **inductive case**: $w = \mathbf{0}x$ for some string $x$. *(Note: Since $|x| < |w|$, we may apply the induction hypothesis to $x$.)* Put the following steps in the correct order to prove this case. $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(w))) = \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{0}x))) \quad$ (because $w = \mathbf{0}x$) $= \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x))) \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{0}x) = \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x)$) $= \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))) \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$: $\mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{1}y) = \mathbf{01} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(y)$) $= 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathsf{grow}(x))) \quad$ (by definition of $\#$: $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{01} \cdot z) = 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, z)$) $\geq 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(x)) \quad$ (by induction hypothesis applied to $x$) $= 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{1} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x)) \quad$ (by definition of $\#$: $\#(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{1} \cdot z) = \#(\mathbf{0}, z)$) $= 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{0}x)) \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$) $= 1 + \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(w)) \quad$ (because $w = \mathbf{0}x$) $= \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(\mathbf{0} \cdot \mathsf{grow}(x))) \quad$ (by definition of $\mathsf{grow}$) $= \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(x)) \quad$ (by induction hypothesis) $\geq \#(\mathbf{0}, \mathsf{grow}(w)) \quad$ (since $1 + k \geq k$ for all $k \geq 0$)
