Define $\mathit{Obliviate}(w)$ as $w$ with all $\mathtt{1}$s deleted (keeping only the $\mathtt{0}$s). Define $T(L) = \{\mathit{Obliviate}(w) \mid w \in L\}$. Let $L$ be the language of all strings over $\{0,1\}$ with an **even number of $\mathtt{0}$s and odd length**. If $w \in L$ has $k$ zeros and $m$ ones (with $k$ even and $k + m$ odd, so $m$ is odd), then $\mathit{Obliviate}(w) = \mathtt{0}^k$ where $k$ is even. Therefore: $$T(L) = \{\mathtt{0}^k \mid k \text{ is even}\} = (\mathtt{00})^*$$ Construct a DFA for $(\mathtt{00})^*$.
