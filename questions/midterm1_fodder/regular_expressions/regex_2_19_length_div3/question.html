Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid |w| \equiv 0 \pmod{3} \}$$ That is, all strings whose length is divisible by $3$. For example, $\varepsilon$, $000$, $111$, $010$, and $101101$ are in the language, but $0$, $01$, $1111$, and $11011$ are not.
((0+1)(0+1)(0+1))* The length is divisible by 3 iff the string can be grouped into blocks of exactly 3 characters.
