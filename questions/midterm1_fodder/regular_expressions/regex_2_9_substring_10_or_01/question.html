Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid w \text{ contains the substring } \mathtt{10} \text{ or the substring } \mathtt{01} \}$$ As an example, $01$, $10$, $0100$, and $110$ are in the language, but $\varepsilon$, $0$, $111$, and $000$ are not.
(0+1)*(01+10)(0+1)* What strings contain neither $01$ nor $10$? Strings with no adjacent pair of different characters — i.e., all $0$s or all $1$s. The language is everything else.
