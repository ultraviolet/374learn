Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid w \text{ does not contain the substring } 010 \}$$ As an example, $\varepsilon$, $0$, $01$, $011$, and $110$ are in the language, but $010$, $1010$, and $0010$ are not.
(1*00*11)*1*(e+00*+00*1) Think about what configurations are "safe." After reading $01$, the next character cannot be $0$ — it must be $1$ or the string must end. Use states to track whether you've seen a prefix of $010$. Build a DFA with states for: no prefix seen, seen $0$, seen $01$. The regex follows from the DFA's structure: runs of $1$s can appear anywhere, but after any block of $0$s you need $11$ before the next $0$ block.
