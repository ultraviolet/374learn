Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \mathtt{0}^*\mathtt{1}\mathtt{0}^* \mid |w| \equiv 0 \pmod{3} \}$$ That is, all strings of the form $0^a \mathtt{1} 0^b$ (exactly one $\mathtt{1}$, surrounded by any $\mathtt{0}$s) whose total length $a + 1 + b$ is divisible by $3$. For example, $\mathtt{001}$, $\mathtt{010}$, $\mathtt{100}$, $\mathtt{000100}$, and $\mathtt{010000}$ are in the language, but $\mathtt{1}$, $\mathtt{01}$, $\mathtt{10}$, and $\mathtt{0010}$ are not.
