Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \mathtt{0}^*\mathtt{1}^* \mid |w| \equiv 0 \pmod{3} \}$$ That is, all strings of the form $0^a 1^b$ (some $0$s followed by some $1$s) whose total length $a + b$ is divisible by $3$. For example, $\varepsilon$, $000$, $111$, $011$, $001$, $000111$, and $001111$ are in the language, but $0$, $01$, $001100$ (wrong form), and $0011$ are not.
(000)*(111)*+0(000)*11(111)*+00(000)*1(111)* The string has the form $0^a 1^b$ with $a + b \equiv 0 \pmod 3$. Consider $a \bmod 3$ and $b \bmod 3$: the pairs $(a, b) \bmod 3$ satisfying $a + b \equiv 0$ are $(0,0)$, $(1,2)$, and $(2,1)$. Case $(a \equiv 0, b \equiv 0)$: $(000)^*(111)^*$. Case $(a \equiv 1, b \equiv 2)$: $0 \cdot (000)^* \cdot 11 \cdot (111)^*$. Case $(a \equiv 2, b \equiv 1)$: $00 \cdot (000)^* \cdot 1 \cdot (111)^*$.
