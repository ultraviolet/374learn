Assume we have the alphabet $\Sigma = \{0, 1\}$. Provide a regular expression for the following language: $$\{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid w \text{ contains the subsequence } 010 \}$$ Recall that a **subsequence** is obtained by deleting zero or more characters (not necessarily contiguous). For example, $010$, $0110$, $10100$, and $01001$ are in the language (they each contain $0$, then $1$, then $0$, not necessarily adjacent), but $01$ and $110$ are not.
